# Project 01 — E-commerce Store Management

## Overview

This project demonstrates my experience supporting and managing online marketplace operations across Lazada, Shopee, and TikTok Shop.

## Main Responsibilities

### Product Listings
- Add and update product information
- Maintain product titles, descriptions, prices, and other listing details
- Check that listings contain the necessary information

### Orders
- Monitor incoming orders
- Track order status
- Help make sure orders are processed on time
- Coordinate when order-related issues need attention

### Inventory
- Monitor product availability
- Check stock levels
- Coordinate stock-related concerns with the warehouse

### Customer Support
- Respond to customer inquiries
- Assist with order-related questions
- Help address concerns about products and orders

### Promotions
- Monitor promotions, vouchers, and discounts
- Help maintain promotional information on store listings
- Check promotional activities during campaigns

### Sales Monitoring
- Monitor store sales and orders
- Review basic sales activity
- Keep track of store performance information

### Warehouse & Delivery Coordination
- Coordinate order and stock concerns with warehouse operations
- Follow up on delivery-related issues when needed

This project demonstrates practical experience with marketplace operations, attention to product and order details, customer communication, and coordination between online stores and fulfillment operations.
