# Project 04 — Product Research Sample

## Purpose

A simple framework for reviewing products before listing or promoting them in an online marketplace.

## Research Areas

| Area | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Product Demand | Customer interest and marketplace activity |
| Competitors | Similar products and competing listings |
| Pricing | Comparable selling prices |
| Product Information | Titles, descriptions, variations, and images |
| Promotions | Discounts, vouchers, and campaign opportunities |
| Stock | Availability and replenishment needs |

## Example Research Notes

When reviewing a product, I would compare similar marketplace listings, check pricing, review how competitors present the product, and organize the information before deciding how the product should be listed or promoted.

## Important Note

The figures and examples in this portfolio are **sample data created for demonstration purposes**. They are not presented as actual company sales figures.
